Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Hermes Agent is a personal-use application with a single user: its developer. It accesses the developer's own Google Account data (Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Sheets, Google Docs, Google Contacts) through official Google APIs to perform personal automation tasks.

Data collection

The application does not collect, aggregate, sell, or share any user data with third parties. There are no analytics, no telemetry, and no third-party services.

Data storage

OAuth tokens are stored locally on the developer's own machine. Email, calendar, and file data is processed only as needed to perform the requested task and is not retained beyond that.

Contact

Questions: the developer (single user) may be reached via the user support email listed on the OAuth consent screen.
